## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧭 [Scout](https://github.com/arya-pradhan/Scout) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `AI study copilot that lives in your texts`

> An AI assistant any college student can text on iMessage. Tracks homework, campus events and job openings, and texts first when something matters. Built for the **TinyFish × Photon** bounty.

`TypeScript` `Node.js` `Claude` `TinyFish` `Photon Spectrum`

- Crawls professors' own course sites and PDFs for due dates that **Canvas never shows**, for any school, with ~3 minutes of onboarding over text
- Claude with **10+ custom tools**: homework lookup, job search, reminders, application tracking, all answered with sourced links
- Read-only on the web by design: it never submits, applies or signs in on your behalf

[🌐 Showcase site](https://scout-site-eight.vercel.app) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [💻 Source](https://github.com/arya-pradhan/Scout)

---

### 🅿️ [ParkWise](https://github.com/arya-pradhan/ParkWise) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Parking detection that runs entirely in your browser`

> Find open parking spaces in a photo or video. A YOLOv5 model runs client-side, so there is no server, no upload and no cost.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `YOLOv5` `ONNX Runtime Web` `WebGPU` `Vitest` `Playwright`

- Trained a custom YOLOv5 model on **250+ labeled images**, telling open from occupied spaces **92%** of the time, with a transparent model card
- Rebuilt a 2022 AI Camp Flask prototype as a fully static Next.js app, with unit and end-to-end tests guarding the detection pipeline
- Slider changes re-filter results in milliseconds without re-running the model

[🌐 Live demo](https://park-wise-olive.vercel.app) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [💻 Source](https://github.com/arya-pradhan/ParkWise)

### 🏥 UNC CRSO Research Assistant &nbsp;·&nbsp; `RAG assistant for clinical research operations`

> An AI research-operations assistant for UNC's Office of Clinical Research Support & Operations, serving **5,300+ active studies** across UNC-Chapel Hill and UNC Health. I lead the 5-person team.

`RAG` `Embeddings` `LLMs` `Python`

- Parses, chunks and embeds SOPs, IRB guidance, institutional policies and training materials
- **Citation-backed answers** that deep-link to the source SOP, policy page or training module
- Role-aware prompting and retrieval for investigators, coordinators, regulatory specialists and monitors
